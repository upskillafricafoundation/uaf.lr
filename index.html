Every Child Deserves a Desk, a Uniform, and a Future.

Every sunrise in Liberia brings a quiet tragedy for thousands of marginalized children who will never step inside a classroom today. While others learn to read and calculate, small hands are forced onto crowded streets selling cold water between speeding cars, breaking rocks in gravel pits, or sitting in dimly lit rooms because their families cannot afford a $1 enrollment fee or basic exercise books.

At Upskill Africa Foundation, we refuse to accept that poverty should dictate a child’s destiny. Under the No Invisible Child (NIC) initiative, our field verifiers walk door-to-door through informal settlements and remote towns to find these forgotten learners. Your generous donation pays school tuition, purchases durable uniforms and textbooks, and trains struggling mothers with sustainable livelihood skills so their households can permanently support their children’s education.

“When you educate a child, you rescue a family from generational silence. With just $1 or $5, you restore dignity, open school doors, and prove to a vulnerable child that they are not invisible.”

— Upskill Africa Foundation Field Verification Team

Intervention Framework

UAF Programs: Strategic & Impact Statements

Each UAF program combines a clear operational mandate with measurable long-term community transformation.

🎒

No Invisible Child (NIC 2026/2027)

Flagship Educational Initiative
Program Statement

Deploy trained community focal verifiers to conduct continuous door-to-door censuses across marginalized settlements in Liberia, identifying, profiling, and removing financial barriers for every child excluded from the formal school system.

Impact Statement

Ensures zero out-of-school child remains unaccounted for; successfully returns over 1,240 vulnerable children into partner primary classrooms equipped with uniforms, shoes, textbooks, and termly attendance tracking.

🏫

Education Access & Retention

Access & Equity
Program Statement

Establish structural subsidies, tuition waivers, and learning material sponsorships directly with community partner schools to guarantee sustained child retention and prevent vulnerable learners from dropping out.

Impact Statement

Eliminates the financial threshold for low-income households, achieving a 92% school term completion rate among supported learners and reducing out-of-school dropouts across monitored partner schools.

🧼

Women & Household Livelihoods

Economic Self-Reliance
Program Statement

Deliver intensive vocational training in artisan bar soap formulation, liquid detergents, tie-dye textiles, and community micro-savings cooperatives (VSLA) for vulnerable mothers and female heads of households.

Impact Statement

Over 680 women certified and operating neighborhood micro-businesses, creating sustainable daily household incomes that independently finance school fees and books for more than 1,800 children.

💻

Alternative Learning Program (ALP)

Digital Inclusion
Program Statement

Provide tuition-free community computer lab bootcamps, typing instruction, spreadsheet accounting, document publishing, and digital literacy for out-of-school adolescents and young mothers.

Impact Statement

Graduated 450+ digitally skilled youths and young mothers, bridging the technological divide and transitioning previously excluded adolescents into formal office apprenticeships and small business administration.

🌱

Youth Development & Innovation

Civic Leadership
Program Statement

Mobilize neighborhood youth councils, civic problem-solving incubators, and peer mentorship networks to direct adolescent energy toward positive community transformation and ethical leadership.

Impact Statement

Diverted 890+ youth from street risks and idle vulnerability into active community focal work, disaster response, and neighborhood census verification across high-density settlements.

🚀

Student Career Development & Mentorship

Future Readiness
Program Statement

Provide structured high school career counseling, university entrance guidance, professional shadowing, and vocational apprenticeships to empower secondary students from underserved backgrounds.

Impact Statement

Guided 720+ secondary students toward technical certifications, higher education scholarships, and dignified careers, providing a clear roadmap from basic education to economic independence.

🛡️

Child Protection & Safeguarding

Safeguarding & Security
Program Statement

Institutionalize zero-tolerance child safeguarding frameworks, establish community grievance reporting desks, and train partner school faculties on positive discipline and child rights protection.

Impact Statement

15 partner communities and over 40 schools certified under strict child protection standards, creating safe learning environments free from violence, exploitation, and illegal child labor.

⚖️

Rights Advocacy & Field Census

Policy & Research
Program Statement

Champion open civic dialogue with community elders, town chiefs, and the Ministry of Education, utilizing independent field evidence to advocate for pro-poor education reforms and rural budget priorities.

Impact Statement

Empowers community elders and local parent associations with verified census data, directly influencing local educational policy and ensuring marginalized communities are prioritized in resource allocation.

Institutional Request

Request Data & Evidence

Transparency without compromising child privacy. Request verified research datasets, program evaluations, or field evidence.

🔒 UAF enforces child safeguarding: individual child identifying records are never disclosed. Only verified, aggregated indicators are released.
