Strategic Alliances

UAF Partners & Collaborators

Working hand-in-hand with community leaders, civil society organizations, schools, and institutional supporters to advance child rights and education access.

Community School Alliances
Education Access Partner

Partnering with verified community primary and junior high schools across Montserrado County to admit out-of-school learners with waived or subsidized fees.

Liberia Child Protection Network
Safeguarding Alliance

Collaborating on child protection referrals, household counseling, and community awareness against child labor and early school drop-outs.

Learning Alternative Program (ALP)
Digital Literacy & Skills

Empowering youth and young mothers with computer literacy, job readiness, and technology training to fund household educational needs.

Community Leadership Councils
Local Governance

Zone leaders, block chairs, and community elders who guide UAF field verifiers through neighborhoods to identify every out-of-school child.
