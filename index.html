Flagship initiative

No Invisible Child

"No child should remain invisible to the education system." UAF's No Invisible Child Initiative (NIC 2026/2027) works across 7 core phases:

01

Identify children outside education.

02

Document the barriers they face.

03

Understand household and community circumstances.

04

Advocate for education access.

05

Support re-enrollment.

06

Support retention.

07

Monitor progress.

What "out of school" means at UAF

A child UAF has assessed as not currently attending or enrolled in a learning program, based on direct verification in the community.

Methodology note, not a statistic

Why re-enrollment support matters

Barriers that keep a child out of school rarely disappear on their own — UAF pairs advocacy with practical, household-level support to help a child return and stay in school.
