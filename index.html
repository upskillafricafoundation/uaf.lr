Every Child Deserves a Desk, a Uniform, and a Future.

Every sunrise in Liberia brings a quiet tragedy for thousands of marginalized children who will never step inside a classroom today. While others learn to read and calculate, small hands are forced onto crowded streets selling cold water between speeding cars, breaking rocks in gravel pits, or sitting in dimly lit rooms because their families cannot afford a $1 enrollment fee or basic exercise books.

At Upskill Africa Foundation, we refuse to accept that poverty should dictate a child’s destiny. Under the No Invisible Child (NIC) initiative, our field verifiers walk door-to-door through informal settlements and remote towns to find these forgotten learners. Your generous donation pays school tuition, purchases durable uniforms and textbooks, and trains struggling mothers with sustainable livelihood skills so their households can permanently support their children’s education.

“When you educate a child, you rescue a family from generational silence. With just $1 or $5, you restore dignity, open school doors, and prove to a vulnerable child that they are not invisible.”

Upskill Africa Foundation Verification Team

UAF Programs

Our programs are designed to promote education access, child protection, skills development, youth empowerment, and household resilience. Through community engagement, advocacy, training, mentorship, and partnerships, we work to reach vulnerable children, youth, women, and households and create practical pathways toward education, opportunity, and sustainable development.

No Invisible Child

Door-to-door child identification, enrollment subsidization, and uniform/kit distribution (NIC 2026/2027).
